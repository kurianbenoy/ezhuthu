FONTLOG for Ezhuthu Kamalam
==========================

Ezhuthu Kamalam is an aperture-variable Malayalam display family. It is based
on the Manjari skeleton (OFL 1.1) and adds a new Petal/Aperture transformation,
a variable APER axis, and a COLRv1 blue→orange Spectrum build.

2026-11-09 — v1.000 (Kamalam)
-----------------------------
- First production release of Ezhuthu Kamalam.
- Variable Regular and Bold fonts with the custom APER (Aperture) axis,
  0 = round, 50 = bloom, 100 = petal (default).
- Point-compatible masters generated by a capped petal warp; static Round,
  Petal and Bold Petal instances included.
- Ezhuthu Kamalam Spectrum: COLRv1/CPAL blue→orange gradient builds in
  Regular (913 colour glyphs) and Bold (907 colour glyphs), both with a
  7-colour palette.
- 1000 UPM, full Malayalam block + Latin, Manjari GSUB/GPOS preserved.
- New metrics: typo 800/-300, win 1100/700, USE_TYPO_METRICS.
- Self-contained HTML specimen, hero/axis/gradient/glyph-sheet PNG proofs.
- Tests: axes, shaping across APER, coverage, colour tables, legacy draft.

2023-?? — v0.2 (legacy draft)
-----------------------------
- Metric correction, letterform tuning, OTF/WOFF2 output, Makefile.
- Full Malayalam block coverage and working OpenType shaping.

2023-?? — v0.1 (legacy draft)
-----------------------------
- Initial from-scratch parametric Malayalam typeface: exact outline
  primitives, UFO generation, GSUB/GPOS via the vendored Nupuram engine.
